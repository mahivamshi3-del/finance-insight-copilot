# Finance Variance Analysis Skill

## Purpose

Analyze financial performance using actual, budget, and prior-period
financial data and produce concise management-level commentary.

## Inputs

The workflow may receive:

- Current revenue
- Previous-period revenue
- Budget revenue
- COGS
- Operating expenses
- Gross profit
- Operating profit
- Profit margin
- Revenue growth
- Budget variance
- Expense ratio

## Analysis Workflow

1. Validate that all required numerical inputs are available.
2. Compare current revenue with previous-period revenue.
3. Compare actual revenue with budget revenue.
4. Analyze gross profit and operating profit.
5. Review profit margin and expense ratio.
6. Identify material positive or negative variances.
7. Explain the financial impact using only the supplied data.
8. Produce concise management commentary.

## Required Output

Return the analysis using these sections:

### 1. Overall Performance

Summarize the overall financial performance.

### 2. Revenue Trend

Explain the change in revenue compared with the previous period.

### 3. Profitability

Explain gross profit, operating profit, and profit margin.

### 4. Budget Variance

Explain whether actual revenue is above or below budget and quantify
the variance.

### 5. Expense Observation

Comment on the operating expense ratio.

### 6. Management Observations

Provide two concise observations that management may consider.

## Rules

- Use only the financial information provided.
- Never invent financial figures.
- Preserve the original units and currency.
- Show calculations clearly when required.
- Distinguish calculated facts from management observations.
- Do not provide investment, tax, legal, or accounting advice.
- If required information is missing, state that it is missing.
- Keep the final management commentary concise and professional.
- Financial outputs should be traceable to the supplied inputs.

## Example Use Case

Input:

- Current Revenue: ₹12,000,000
- Previous Revenue: ₹10,000,000
- Budget Revenue: ₹11,000,000
- COGS: ₹6,000,000
- Operating Expenses: ₹2,500,000

Output should explain:

- Revenue growth
- Budget variance
- Gross profit
- Operating profit
- Profit margin
- Expense ratio
- Key management observations
